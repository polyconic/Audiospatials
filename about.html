About.

Audiospatials is the collaborative project of Gregor Egan and Hunter Bowersmith - two musicians whose paths crossed at a party in Santa Cruz, CA, where they bonded over a mutual love for experimental sounds and genre-defying music.

Gregor, a Chicago-born and trained jazz pianist, began producing IDM and experimental electronic music in 2020. His work gradually evolved into dark and hypnotic techno. He is based in LA.

Hunter, a self-taught guitarist, is the creative force behind FENNEC, a project that blends rock, folk, and electronic sounds. He is currently based in Santa Cruz, California.

Audiospatials was born to create a space where live instrumentation, music production, mixing, and mastering for all artists comes together. Creative direction in all forms.

Visuospatials is the design and visual identity behind the artists.
